How to play

  1. Board: two rows of five pits plus a big Quan pit at each end. Player 1 owns the bottom row, Player 2 the top row. Each small pit starts with 5 commoner stones; each Quan holds 1 mandarin stone worth 10.
  2. Your move: click one of your five pits that has stones, then choose a direction — ← Left or Right → (stones travel toward that side of the board).
  3. Stones are sown one at a time into the following pits, including the Quan pits.
  4. If the pit right after your last stone is a normal pit with stones, scoop them all up and keep sowing.
  5. If that next pit is a Quan still holding commoners, take one commoner from it and keep sowing — the mandarin stone stays. A Quan with no commoners left ends your turn.
  6. If that pit is empty and the one after it has stones, you capture those stones (and keep capturing while the empty→full pattern repeats). Capturing a mandarin scores 10.
  7. Two empty pits in a row end your turn.
  8. If all your pits are empty when your turn starts, pay 5 points to refill them — if you can't, you lose.
  9. The game ends when both Quan pits are completely empty (mandarin captured and no commoners left). Final score = points captured + stones remaining on your side; highest score wins.
